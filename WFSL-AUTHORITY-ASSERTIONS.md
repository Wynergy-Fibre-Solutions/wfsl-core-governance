# WFSL Authority Assertions

## Purpose
Declare the explicit assertions under which WFSL authority
is claimed, verified, and relied upon.

## Assertions
- Authority exists only where explicitly sealed
- Signed tags constitute authoritative intent
- Verification must be deterministic and reproducible
- Registry presence is mandatory for authority claims

## Non-Assertions
- Possession does not imply authority
- Forking does not transfer authority
- Documentation alone does not confer rights

## Validation
- wfsl-verify.ps1 provides the canonical authority check
- Negative proof demonstrates absence of authority

## Reliance
- Third parties may rely on verified assertions only
- No implied warranties beyond sealed scope
