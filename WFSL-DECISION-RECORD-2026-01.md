WFSL has sealed Media Integrity Charter v1.0 using signed,
verifiable governance artefacts bound to cryptographic authority.

Authority is enforced through explicit verification and registry
presence rather than file possession or replication.

This level of hardening is sufficient for licensing, transfer,
audit, and demonstration. Further complexity would not increase
material value.

WFSL therefore considers this workstream complete and proceeds
to the next phase.
