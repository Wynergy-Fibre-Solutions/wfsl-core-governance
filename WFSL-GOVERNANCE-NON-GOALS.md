# WFSL Governance Non-Goals

## Purpose
Explicitly state what the WFSL Governance Core does not
attempt to achieve.

## Non-Goals
- Legal adjudication or enforcement
- Regulatory compliance substitution
- Operational process control
- Software execution or automation
- Real-time monitoring or enforcement

## Clarifications
- Governance artefacts provide structure, not outcomes
- Authority verification confirms origin, not correctness
- Compliance mapping is illustrative, not prescriptive

## Rationale
Stating non-goals prevents scope creep, misinterpretation,
and inappropriate reliance.
