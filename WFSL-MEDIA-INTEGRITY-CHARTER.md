# WFSL Media Integrity Proof Charter v1.0

## Status
ACTIVE – PUBLIC CHARTER

## Purpose
WFSL provides deterministic, evidence-based verification for digital images and audio in an era of AI-generated and manipulated media.

WFSL does not decide truth.
WFSL verifies artefacts.

The objective is to stabilise communities by replacing speculation with verifiable evidence.

---

## Scope of Verification

WFSL may generate proof regarding whether a media artefact is:

1. Integrity-preserved since first WFSL observation
2. Consistent with unmodified natural capture
3. Consistent with known synthetic or AI construction classes
4. Altered, recompressed, or post-processed after creation
5. Derived from, or linked to, a previously observed artefact

All verification outputs are:
- Deterministic
- Repeatable
- Evidence-sealed
- Time-anchored

---

## Explicit Non-Claims (Hard Boundaries)

WFSL does not and will never assert:

- Authorship or creator identity
- Intent or motive
- Truthfulness or falsity of depicted events
- Whether a person is real, impersonated, or fabricated
- Absolute certainty beyond declared confidence bands

These boundaries are permanent and non-negotiable.

---

## Confidence Model

WFSL publishes confidence bands, not absolutes.

- Very High Confidence  
  Integrity verification, tamper detection, derivation linkage

- High Confidence  
  Consistency with known synthetic or AI construction classes

- Out of Scope  
  Truth, belief, narrative interpretation, intent, or identity

Confidence is contextual and explicitly stated with each result.

---

## Evidence Handling Principles

- Media artefacts are normalised into canonical form
- Multiple evidential hashes and feature signatures are generated
- Evidence bundles are cryptographically sealed
- Original artefacts are never modified
- No editorial interpretation is applied

WFSL preserves evidence.
WFSL does not reinterpret reality.

---

## Community Protection Statement

WFSL exists to reduce anxiety caused by synthetic and manipulated media by providing calm, verifiable, repeatable proof.

WFSL does not tell communities what to believe.
WFSL gives communities evidence about the artefact itself.

---

## Public Operating Statement

WFSL provides independent media integrity proof for images and audio in an age of AI-generated content.
We verify artefacts, not narratives, so people can make informed decisions without fear or coercion.
