# WFSL Governance Boundaries

## Purpose
Define the explicit boundaries of authority, scope,
and responsibility within the WFSL Governance Core.

## Authority Boundary
- Governance authority is asserted only through signed tags
- Verification is external, deterministic, and registry-bound
- No implied authority exists outside sealed artefacts

## Scope Boundary
Included:
- Governance documents
- Release manifests
- Authority verification artefacts

Excluded:
- Business operations
- Software execution
- Infrastructure control
- Legal enforcement

## Responsibility Boundary
- WFSL governs artefacts it seals
- Downstream users bear responsibility for application
- No delegation of authority by possession

## Change Boundary
- Forward-only evolution
- Superseding releases require explicit linkage
- No retroactive modification

## State
Active.
