\# WFSL Public Trust and Safety Statement



\## Purpose



This statement explains how WFSL software protects its users, operators, and downstream systems in an era of increasing cyber risk.



WFSL systems are designed to be safe to inspect, safe to operate, and safe to rely upon.



---



\## Our Commitment



WFSL commits to building software that:



\- Does not hide its behaviour

\- Does not enforce silently

\- Does not alter systems without consent

\- Does not claim trust without proof



Trust is earned through evidence, not promises.



---



\## How WFSL Protects Users



\### 1. Evidence-Based Trust



Every WFSL system can produce cryptographic evidence of its state and behaviour.



This evidence is:

\- Append-only

\- Time-stamped

\- Cryptographically signed

\- Independently verifiable



Users do not need to “take our word for it”.



---



\### 2. No Silent Enforcement



WFSL software never enforces rules without explicit, visible consent.



\- Observe-only mode is the default

\- Enforcement must be declared

\- Enforcement can be inspected before use



This protects users from unexpected failures.



---



\### 3. Human-Safe Failure Model



WFSL systems are designed to fail safely.



\- Unknown states are never treated as failure

\- No automatic remediation

\- No destructive actions

\- No hidden side effects



Errors are reported, not acted upon.



---



\### 4. Cryptographic Integrity



WFSL evidence records are protected using modern cryptography.



\- SHA-256 content addressing

\- Ed25519 digital signatures

\- Repo-local trust anchors



Any tampering is detectable.



---



\### 5. Independent Verification



WFSL evidence can be verified by third parties using standard tools.



Verification does not require:

\- WFSL infrastructure

\- WFSL servers

\- Special permissions



This protects auditors, partners, and regulators.



---



\## What WFSL Does Not Do



WFSL does not:



\- Monitor users without disclosure

\- Transmit telemetry covertly

\- Exfiltrate data

\- Rewrite history

\- Delete evidence to hide mistakes



WFSL systems are designed to withstand scrutiny.



---



\## Responsibility and Use



While WFSL provides strong technical safeguards:



\- Users remain responsible for how software is deployed

\- Verification is always recommended

\- Trust claims should be checked, not assumed



WFSL provides tools for verification, not blind trust.



---



\## Legal and Ethical Position



WFSL software is designed to be deployable in:



\- Regulated environments

\- High-risk infrastructure

\- Sensitive operational contexts



WFSL prioritises human safety, transparency, and accountability.



---



\## Closing Statement



WFSL believes that security without transparency is fragile.



By combining:

\- Deterministic systems

\- Cryptographic evidence

\- Explicit policy

\- Human-first defaults



WFSL aims to protect not just software, but the people who depend on it.



This statement is part of the WFSL Cyber-Trust Doctrine.
