# WFSL Licence Addendum

This licence applies to the repository wfsl-core-governance
as anchored at tag governance-verifier-v1.1.

Authority is defined by:
- The signed governance tag
- The WFSL signing key (EDDSA)
- The WFSL trust registry

Licence scopes may include:
- Verify-only
- Audit-only
- Publish-authority

Authority transfer and revocation are forward-only.
Historical artefacts remain valid.
