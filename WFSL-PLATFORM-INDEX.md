# WFSL Platform Index

## Purpose
This index defines the official WFSL platform structure.
It identifies platform boundaries, guarantees, and repository membership,
and acts as the authoritative reference for users, partners, and acquirers.

WFSL platforms are composable, lawful, and internally convergent by design.

---

## Platform 1 — WFSL Evidence Platform

### Purpose
Provide deterministic, evidence-only tooling for integrity, verification,
and long-term trust without attribution or surveillance.

### Core Repositories
- wfsl-shared-verifier
- wfsl-proofgate-cli

### Guarantees
- Evidence-only processing
- Deterministic outputs
- Cryptographic hash verification
- No identity attribution
- No behavioural inference
- Artefacts verifiable indefinitely

### Intended Users
- Engineers
- Auditors
- Compliance and assurance teams
- Telecom and infrastructure operators
- GovTech contractors

---

## Platform 2 — WFSL Verification Platform

### Purpose
Provide verifiable proof of software, repository, and system state
aligned with governance and licence boundaries.

### Core Repositories
- wfsl-repo-guard
- wfsl-proofgate-cli

### Guarantees
- Repository state verification
- Build and output integrity
- Licence compatibility checks
- Governance compliance signalling
- Deterministic status artefacts

### Intended Users
- Software teams
- Platform operators
- Infrastructure providers
- Security and compliance reviewers

---

## Platform 3 — WFSL Trust Core Platform

### Purpose
Provide the governance, licensing, and trust primitives that enable
WFSL platforms to compose safely and lawfully.

### Core Components
- WFSL Non-Goals Declaration
- WFSL Licence Escalation Matrix
- WFSL Trust Transfer Model
- WFSL Continuity and Exit Principles
- WFSL Platform Compatibility Contract
- Cyber inheritance manifests

### Guarantees
- Lawful convergence across platforms
- Explicit cyber and licence boundaries
- No vendor lock-in
- User sovereignty
- Deterministic trust transfer

### Intended Users
- Platform builders
- Enterprise adopters
- Regulators and auditors
- Strategic partners and acquirers

---

## Platform Composition Rules

- Platforms may be used independently or together
- When composed, the strictest guarantee applies
- No platform may weaken another platform’s guarantees
- Licence and cyber boundaries must remain intact

Composition that violates these rules is invalid.
