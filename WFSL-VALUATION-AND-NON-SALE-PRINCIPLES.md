Valuation is a measure of authority, not liquidity.

- Build-to-sell strategies
- Acquisition optimisation
- Artificial revenue inflation

- Establish negotiating power
- Protect against hostile influence
- Anchor strategic discussions

Valuation is defensive, not aspirational.

WFSL value is derived from:

- Control-plane ownership
- Deterministic governance IP
- Verification standards
- Cross-system enforceability

These assets compound without sales.

- Modular governance primitives
- Cross-domain applicability

- It is systemically consistent
- It requires discipline over time
- It embeds intent, not just code

Replication cost exceeds acquisition cost.

- Replacement engineering cost
- Governance design depth
- Strategic deterrence value

This establishes a non-negotiable floor.
