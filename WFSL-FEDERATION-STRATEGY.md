# WFSL Federation Strategy

Entity: Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  
Document Type: Federation Strategy  
Status: Active and Binding

---

## 1. Federation Purpose

This document defines how WFSL federates with external systems, organisations, and operators without surrendering authority.

Federation is a controlled extension of WFSL systems, not a dilution of governance.

---

## 2. Federation Principle

WFSL federates by **interface**, not by trust.

No external party is trusted implicitly.  
All federation occurs through governed, observable, and enforceable boundaries.

---

## 3. Non-Surrender of Authority

WFSL does not delegate or outsource core authority.

Federation does not permit:
- Override of WFSL governance
- Bypass of guards or evidence requirements
- Silent policy substitution
- External control planes

WFSL remains sovereign at all times.

---

## 4. Federation Eligibility

An external entity may federate with WFSL only if it can:

- Accept WFSL admission controls
- Produce verifiable execution evidence
- Operate under deterministic constraints
- Tolerate rejection or revocation without negotiation

Inability to meet these conditions disqualifies federation.

---

## 5. Federation Modes

WFSL recognises controlled federation modes, including:

### 5.1 Tooling Federation
External tools interact with WFSL systems through controlled APIs or interfaces.

No tool receives implicit privilege.

### 5.2 Operator Federation
External operators may execute WFSL-governed workflows under admission and guard enforcement.

Identity does not confer authority.

### 5.3 System Federation
External systems may interoperate with WFSL components through evidence-governed interfaces.

State synchronisation is explicit and auditable.

---

## 6. Admission and Revocation

All federation relationships are subject to:

- Explicit admission
- Defined scope
- Instrumentation requirements
- Continuous verification

WFSL retains unilateral revocation rights.

Revocation does not require justification beyond governance breach or risk.

---

## 7. Evidence and Audit

Federated activity must emit evidence equivalent to internal WFSL execution.

Federated execution without evidence is treated as non-execution.

Audit rights are retained by WFSL at all times.

---

## 8. Performance and Impact Controls

Federated systems must not degrade WFSL performance or integrity.

WFSL may:
- Throttle federated activity
- Isolate federated execution
- Terminate federation on performance risk

Performance impact is a governance concern.

---

## 9. Federation Evolution

Federation arrangements are versioned and reviewable.

Changes require:
- Explicit update
- Compatibility assessment
- Governance review

Implicit evolution is prohibited.

---

## 10. Exit and Dissolution

Federation is reversible by design.

On exit:
- Access is terminated
- Interfaces are closed
- Evidence is retained
- No dependency remains

WFSL avoids irreversible coupling.

---

## 11. Final Binding Declaration

This Federation Strategy governs all WFSL federation activity.

Federation without compliance is invalid.
