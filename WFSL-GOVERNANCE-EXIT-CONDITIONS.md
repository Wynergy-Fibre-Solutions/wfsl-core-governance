# WFSL Governance Exit Conditions

## Purpose
Define the conditions under which the WFSL Governance Core
may be formally exited, replaced, or superseded.

## Exit Conditions
- Introduction of a superseding governance release
- Explicit revocation of authority via signed notice
- Organisational dissolution or transfer event

## Requirements
- Exit must be declared via signed artefact
- Prior sealed releases remain historically valid
- Superseding authority must be explicit and verifiable

## Prohibitions
- No silent deprecation
- No implicit withdrawal of authority
- No deletion of historical governance records

## Continuity
In the absence of an explicit exit, the latest sealed
governance release remains authoritative.

## State
Active.
