
# WFSL Governance Succession

## Purpose
Define how governance authority persists across
leadership or structural changes without loss
of integrity or auditability.

## Succession Triggers
- Leadership transition
- Organisational reformation
- Creation of a successor entity

## Succession Rules
- Succession must be declared explicitly
- Declaration must reference the current sealed release
- Successor authority must be identifiable and verifiable

## Continuity
- Historical releases remain valid
- Audit trail is preserved end-to-end
- Verification rules remain unchanged

## Constraints
- No implied succession
- No authority without signed declaration
- No break in verification lineage

## State
Active.
