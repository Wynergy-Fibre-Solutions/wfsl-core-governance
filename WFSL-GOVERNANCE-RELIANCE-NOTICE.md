# WFSL Governance Reliance Notice

## Purpose
Define the conditions under which third parties may
rely on WFSL governance artefacts.

## Reliance Conditions
- Authority must be verified successfully
- Artefacts must be within a sealed release boundary
- No reliance is permitted on unverified copies

## Non-Reliance
- No reliance on forks without authority verification
- No reliance on partial artefact sets
- No reliance beyond documented scope

## Responsibility
- WFSL asserts authority only for sealed artefacts
- Reliance decisions remain with the relying party

## Limitation
This notice limits reliance to verified authority
and does not imply warranty or fitness for purpose.
