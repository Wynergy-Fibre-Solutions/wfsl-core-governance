# WFSL Operating Model

Entity: Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  
Document Type: Operating Model  
Status: Active and Binding

---

## 1. Operating Model Purpose

This document defines how WFSL operates day to day.

It translates constitutional authority, execution covenants, and instrumentation standards into a practical, repeatable operating flow.

The operating model governs how work enters, moves through, and exits WFSL systems.

---

## 2. Governance-First Operation

WFSL operates under governance-first principles.

All operational activity must:
- Align with the WFSL Core Constitution
- Respect ratified doctrines and covenants
- Produce verifiable evidence
- Remain instrumented and observable

Operational convenience never overrides governance.

---

## 3. Work Intake and Admission

All work entering WFSL systems is subject to admission control.

### 3.1 Sources of Work
Work may originate from:
- Internal system evolution
- Field execution requirements
- Verification and testing needs
- External dependency pressure
- Governance mandates

### 3.2 Admission Requirements
No work is admitted unless it has:
- A clear execution purpose
- Defined success and failure states
- Instrumentation expectations
- Evidence requirements

Work without admission metadata is rejected.

---

## 4. Execution Flow

WFSL execution follows a deterministic flow:

1. Admission and classification
2. Guard validation
3. Instrumented execution
4. Evidence emission
5. Verification and review
6. Closure or iteration

Each stage must complete successfully before progression.

---

## 5. Guard-Enforced Operations

All execution stages are enforced by guards.

Guards may:
- Block non-compliant work
- Halt execution on missing evidence
- Reject uncontrolled environments
- Enforce performance and integrity thresholds

Guard decisions are final unless governance intervention is explicitly triggered.

---

## 6. Evidence and Review

Every execution produces evidence.

Evidence is used to:
- Verify correctness
- Assess performance
- Support audits
- Inform governance decisions

Review is evidence-driven, not opinion-driven.

---

## 7. Change and Evolution

WFSL evolves through controlled change.

Changes must be:
- Explicit
- Versioned
- Instrumented
- Reviewed against governance impact

Uncontrolled or silent change is prohibited.

---

## 8. Incident and Failure Handling

Failures are treated as governance events.

On failure:
- Execution halts where required
- Evidence is preserved
- Root cause is assessed
- Remediation is governed and recorded

Failure without evidence is unacceptable.

---

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

Roles within WFSL are functional, not authoritative.

Authority resides in systems and governance artefacts.

Individuals execute within constraints, not discretion.

---

## 10. External Interaction

WFSL interacts with external parties through controlled interfaces.

External dependencies must:
- Be observable
- Be instrumented where possible
- Not override WFSL governance

WFSL does not outsource authority.

---

## 11. Operating Boundaries

WFSL does not:
- Optimise for speed at the cost of integrity
- Prioritise narrative over execution
- Permit ungoverned experimentation in production contexts

Boundaries are enforced, not suggested.

---

## 12. Final Binding Declaration

This Operating Model is mandatory across WFSL.

Deviation constitutes an operational and governance breach.
