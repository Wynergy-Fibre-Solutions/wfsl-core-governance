# Governance Boundaries — WFSL

This document defines the limits of authority, responsibility,
and scope for WFSL governance.

---

## Included

- Corporate governance intent
- Compliance posture
- Decision accountability
- External audit readiness

---

## Excluded

- Operational procedures
- HR policy detail
- Software behaviour
- Automation rules

---

## Boundary Rule

If a decision materially affects safety, compliance,
or legal exposure, it must be documented.

End of document.
