# WFSL Attestation

## Purpose
Provide a formal attestation covering the completeness,
integrity, and authority of the WFSL Governance Core
Silent Release.

## Attestation
WFSL attests that:
- All governance artefacts within scope are present
- Artefacts are sealed under signed authority
- Verification yields deterministic results
- Release boundaries are explicit and enforced

## Basis
This attestation is based on:
- Signed release tags
- Repository history
- Authority verification outcomes

## Limitations
- Applies only to the stated release scope
- No operational or legal assurances are implied
- Reliance requires independent verification

## Validity
Valid for wfsl-governance-silent-release-v1.0
and any explicitly linked superseding releases.
