# WFSL Core Constitution

Entity: Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  
Document Type: Constitutional  
Status: Active and Binding

---

## 1. Constitutional Authority

This document constitutes the highest governing instrument of WFSL.

All WFSL systems, software, governance artefacts, licences, doctrines, and execution models derive authority from this Constitution.

No subordinate document may contradict this Constitution.

---

## 2. Governance Hierarchy

WFSL governance is ordered as follows, from highest to lowest authority:

1. WFSL Core Constitution
2. WFSL Ratification Ledger
3. WFSL Platform Control Doctrine
4. WFSL Execution Covenant
5. WFSL Federation and Operating Instruments
6. WFSL Technical and Enforcement Specifications

Lower-order documents must defer to higher-order instruments.

---

## 3. Ratification Binding

WFSL governance authority is formally instantiated through the WFSL Ratification Ledger.

The following ratification is binding and irreversible by design:

- WFSL Platform Control Doctrine v1.0  
- Ratified via WFSL-RATIFICATION-LEDGER.md  
- Commit reference: 6de50e4  

Ratification establishes system authority through execution and record, not recognition.

---

## 4. Platform Control Doctrine Incorporation

The WFSL Platform Control Doctrine is hereby incorporated into this Constitution by reference.

All WFSL systems must comply with the doctrine’s requirements regarding:

- Operator sovereignty
- Deterministic enablement
- Prohibition of hidden enforcement
- Verifiable disablement
- Separation of host and authority
- Evidence-driven control
- Performance as a governance concern

Non-compliance constitutes a constitutional violation.

---

## 5. System Governance Principle

WFSL is governed by its systems.

Human discretion is subordinate to:
- Deterministic enforcement
- Evidence-based verification
- Sealed governance artefacts

This constraint applies equally to founders, operators, contributors, and future stewards.

---

## 6. Non-Override Clause

No individual, role, or external entity may:

- Suspend constitutional controls
- Override ratified doctrine
- Introduce silent enforcement
- Bypass governance artefacts

All changes must occur through explicit, versioned, and ratified processes.

---

## 7. Continuity and Succession

WFSL governance persists independently of:

- Personnel changes
- Ownership changes
- Market conditions
- External recognition

This Constitution ensures continuity of authority beyond individual control.

---

## 8. Final Declaration

WFSL is constituted as a governance-first, system-authoritative organisation.

This Constitution is binding upon all WFSL activity.
