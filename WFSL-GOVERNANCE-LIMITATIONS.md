# WFSL Governance Limitations

## Purpose
Define explicit limitations of the WFSL Governance Core
to prevent overreach and misinterpretation.

## Limitations
- Governance artefacts do not guarantee correctness
- Verification confirms authority, not quality
- No enforcement beyond cryptographic proof
- No substitution for legal, regulatory, or operational controls

## Boundary Conditions
- Authority applies only to sealed artefacts
- Scope is limited to documented governance
- Reliance requires independent verification

## Implications
- Users must assess suitability independently
- Governance does not imply endorsement or warranty
