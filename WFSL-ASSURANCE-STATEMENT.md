# WFSL Assurance Statement

## Purpose
Provide a concise assurance statement covering the
WFSL Governance Core Silent Release.

## Assurance
WFSL asserts that:
- Governance artefacts are complete for stated scope
- Authority is cryptographically sealed and verifiable
- Release boundaries are explicit and immutable
- Audit and supersession mechanisms are defined

## Limitations
- Assurance applies only to sealed artefacts
- No assurance is given beyond documented scope
- No operational or legal guarantees are implied

## Reliance
Third parties may rely on this assurance only
after successful authority verification.

## Validity
Valid for Silent Release v1.0 and any explicitly
referenced superseding releases.

## State
Active.
