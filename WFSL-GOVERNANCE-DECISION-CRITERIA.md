# WFSL Governance Decision Criteria

## Purpose
Define deterministic criteria used when making
governance decisions within WFSL.

## Criteria
- Alignment with governance principles
- Preservation of authority integrity
- Forward-only impact
- Audit traceability
- Scope containment

## Disallowed Criteria
- Convenience over integrity
- Retroactive justification
- Implicit authority transfer
- Undocumented exceptions

## Application
Criteria apply to:
- Release decisions
- Supersession decisions
- Governance changes

## Authority
WFSL Governance Authority

## State
Active.
