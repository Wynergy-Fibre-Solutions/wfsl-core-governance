# WFSL Risk Register

## Purpose
Identify and record non-operational risks associated with
governance, authority, and release integrity.

## Risks
- Authority key compromise
- Tag misuse or duplication
- Unauthorised fork misrepresentation
- Process drift post-release
- Incomplete archival capture

## Mitigations
- Key lifecycle and rotation policy
- Signed, immutable tags
- Registry-bound verification
- Post-release controls
- Archive manifest enforcement

## Residual Risk
Low, given deterministic verification and forward-only rules.

## Ownership
WFSL Governance Authority

## Review Cycle
On superseding release only.

## State
Active.
