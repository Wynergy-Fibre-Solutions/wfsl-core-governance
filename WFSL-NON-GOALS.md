# WFSL Non-Goals Declaration

## Purpose
This document defines explicit non-goals for Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL).
It exists to prevent capability drift, regulatory ambiguity, and misuse of WFSL
software and platforms.

These non-goals are permanent unless superseded by a formally sealed governance
revision.

---

## Explicit Non-Goals

WFSL will not design, build, market, or enable:

- Surveillance platforms of any kind
- Covert or mass data collection systems
- Behavioural profiling or inference engines
- Identity resolution or attribution systems
- User tracking or fingerprinting mechanisms
- RF, BLE, Wi-Fi, or network fingerprinting for identification
- Packet inspection beyond lawful diagnostics explicitly licensed
- Signal interception or communications monitoring
- Data brokerage, resale, or enrichment businesses
- Predictive policing, social scoring, or risk scoring systems
- Platforms designed to influence, manipulate, or profile individuals

---

## Architectural Intent

WFSL software is designed to provide:
- Deterministic verification
- Evidence integrity
- Lawful testing and diagnostics
- Transparent governance primitives

WFSL software is not designed to observe, infer, or decide on human behaviour.

---

## Boundary Enforcement

If a capability request conflicts with this declaration:
- The request is out of scope for WFSL
- A separate legal entity, licence, and governance framework would be required
- WFSL core platforms will not be extended to support such capability

---

## Permanence

This document applies across:
- All WFSL repositories
- All WFSL platforms
- All WFSL licence bundles
- All WFSL enterprise deployments
