# WFSL Governance Handoff

## Purpose
Define how governance authority and stewardship may be
formally handed off within WFSL without breaking
authority or audit continuity.

## Handoff Conditions
- Organisational restructuring
- Controlled transfer of stewardship
- Formation of a successor governance body

## Requirements
- Handoff declared via signed governance artefact
- Reference to current sealed release tag
- Explicit identification of successor authority

## Constraints
- No implicit handoff
- No loss of historical audit trail
- Prior releases remain valid for their scope

## Verification
Handoff artefacts are subject to the same
deterministic verification rules.

## State
Active.
