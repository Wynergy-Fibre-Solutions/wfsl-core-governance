# WFSL Continuity and Exit Principles

## Purpose
This document defines how WFSL software, licences, and trust guarantees
remain valid and operable in the event that WFSL ceases operations,
changes ownership, or withdraws from active development.

---

## Core Principle

WFSL software must not require WFSL to exist in order to function,
verify, or remain lawful.

No WFSL platform includes hidden dependencies, callbacks, or control
mechanisms that create vendor lock-in.

---

## Operational Independence

WFSL software:
- Runs without WFSL infrastructure
- Does not require WFSL-hosted services
- Does not depend on remote verification
- Produces self-contained artefacts

Users retain full operational control at all times.

---

## Licence Continuity

WFSL licences:
- Remain valid after WFSL exit
- Do not auto-revoke on dissolution
- Do not require renewal for artefact verification
- Survive acquisition, insolvency, or closure

---

## Evidence Durability

Evidence artefacts:
- Remain verifiable indefinitely
- Do not depend on WFSL keys or servers
- Can be independently validated by third parties

---

## No Kill-Switches

WFSL software includes:
- No remote disable mechanisms
- No hidden expiration logic
- No forced update requirements
- No callback enforcement

---

## Acquisition Neutrality

In the event of acquisition:
- Existing guarantees remain intact
- Licences do not silently change scope
- Users retain rights under original terms

Any change requires explicit re-licensing.
